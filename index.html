Actuarial pricing — Auto insurance

Frequency-Severity model on 678,000 real policies (freMTPL2)
Building a complete pricing engine: claims frequency modeling (Negative Binomial GLM), severity modeling (Gamma GLM), benchmark against XGBoost, and conversion into a commercial premium factoring in costs, margin, and reinsurance loading.
Validated study
678Kpolicies analyzed
285,537AIC — Negative Binomial GLM selected
+1.2%XGBoost gain vs GLM (marginal)
±11.5%gap between Pure Premium / actual claims
PythonstatsmodelsXGBoostStreamlit

The choice of a Negative Binomial GLM over Poisson was statistically validated (overdispersion detected, Pearson χ²/df ≈ 2.55). The XGBoost benchmark confirms that classic actuarial risk factors (bonus-malus, vehicle age) already capture most of the signal — a conclusion directly applicable to our client scoring engines.

The convergence between the GLM and Machine Learning on the same dominant risk factors strengthens confidence in the diagnosis — a principle we apply to every model we deliver to clients.

Credit pricing — Basel II/III Scoring & Pricing

Probability of default, LGD, EAD on 150,000 real borrowers
A complete credit pricing engine: Probability of Default (Logistic Regression + XGBoost benchmark), isotonic calibration, documented LGD/EAD, and conversion into a risk-adjusted interest rate under the Basel framework.

A critical calibration bias (4.9x overestimation) was detected and then corrected via isotonic calibration — essential validation as soon as a model feeds into a monetary calculation, not just a binary decision.

Explicitly detecting and correcting a calibration bias reflects the validation rigor expected in a regulated credit decision.
