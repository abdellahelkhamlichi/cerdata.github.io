Actuarial pricing — Motor insurance

Frequency-Severity model on 678,000 real policies (freMTPL2)
Building a complete pricing engine: modelling claim frequency (Negative Binomial GLM) and severity (Gamma GLM), benchmarking against XGBoost, and converting results into a commercial premium including expenses, margin, and reinsurance loading.
Validated study
678Kpolicies analysed
285 537AIC — Negative Binomial GLM selected
+1.2%XGBoost vs GLM gain (marginal)
±11.5%Pure Premium / Actual Claims gap
PythonstatsmodelsXGBoostStreamlit

Choosing the Negative Binomial GLM over Poisson was statistically validated (overdispersion detected, Pearson χ²/df ≈ 2.55). The XGBoost benchmark confirms that traditional actuarial risk factors (bonus-malus, vehicle age) already capture most of the signal — a conclusion directly applicable to our client scoring engines.

The convergence between the GLM and machine learning on the same dominant risk factors increases confidence in the diagnosis — a principle we apply to every model delivered to our clients.
